Bludia provides the backend foundation your applications need — from structured data and APIs to authentication, permissions, background logic, and infrastructure.

Instead of building and maintaining the same backend components for every project, configure them in Bludia and focus on building your product.

Whether you're building a SaaS platform, an internal management system, a booking application, or a mobile app, Bludia gives you a unified backend to work with.

## What is Bludia?

Bludia is a backend infrastructure platform that lets you define, manage, and operate application backends without having to build every backend component from scratch.

Your backend is configured through a structured project environment. You define your data models, relationships, permissions, and business logic, and Bludia provides the infrastructure to make them available through APIs.

Bludia also handles the operational foundation behind your backend, including hosting, deployment, security, and backups.

## How Bludia works

Bludia is built around a simple idea: define your backend once and use it across your applications.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a project">
    Start with a new Bludia project. Each project provides an isolated environment for your application's backend configuration and data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define your data structure">
    Create collections, configure fields, and establish relationships that represent your application's data model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your backend">
    Set up authentication, permissions, API access, computed fields, and custom hooks according to your application's requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your application">
    Use Bludia's REST API to connect your frontend, mobile application, or external service to your backend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy and operate">
    Put your backend into production and manage its infrastructure through Bludia.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Built for different kinds of applications

Bludia is not limited to one type of software. Its backend capabilities can support a wide range of applications.

- **Business applications:** CRMs, management systems, and internal tools.
- **SaaS products:** Multi-feature applications with structured data, authentication, and permissions.
- **Booking platforms:** Reservations, registrations, scheduling, and customer management.
- **Dashboards and portals:** Administrative panels, reporting systems, and customer portals.
- **Mobile applications:** Backends for iOS, Android, and cross-platform applications.

## Who is Bludia for?

Bludia is designed for developers, software teams, and businesses that want to reduce the time and operational overhead involved in building and maintaining application backends.

You can use Bludia alongside your preferred frontend framework, development tools, and workflow. It does not require you to adopt a specific frontend technology.
